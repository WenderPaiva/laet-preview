## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of scientific dermatological precision and holistic luxury wellness. The visual tone is tranquil, quiet, authoritative, and deeply rejuvenating—evoking high-end spa retreats, private aesthetic clinics, and bespoke dermatological rituals.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
- **Editorial Minimalism:** Characterized by generous negative space, intentional typographic pacing, architectural composition, and disciplined restraint.
- **Organic Tactility & Translucency:** Muted warm-sand surfaces layered with frosted-glass accents, delicate hairline structural frames, and luminous ambient radiance.
- **Emotional Intent:** To instill a state of calm confidence, purity, and clinical distinction. Visual noise is eliminated to honor breathing room and refined tactile interaction.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in botanical clays, soft natural sands, and understated gold accents, anchored by deep botanical slate for typographic legibility.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#5B6B5A`, `#7C8D7B`):** Deep Botanical Sage and Restorative Green. Used for primary interactions, prominent icons, focused states, and key branded focal elements.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#D4AF37`, `#C5A059`):** Refined Champagne Gold. Reserved strictly for curated luxury highlights, subtle status badges, treatment tiers, and quiet celebratory moments. Never overused as solid background blocks.
- **Surfaces & Canvases (`#FCFCFB`, `#F7F5F0`, `#EFEAE3`):** Luminous Off-White canvas as the ground layer, transitioning to Soft Sand and Warm Nude for tiered cards, subtle section zoning, and inner wells.
- **Borders & Separators (`#E5E0D8`):** Delicate hairline tone delivering structural definition without stark visual boundaries.
- **Typography & Content:**
  - **High-Contrast Slate (`#1E221E`):** Primary display headers, body text, and sharp iconography.
  - **Muted Olive Gray (`#636B62`):** Secondary metadata, timestamps, input placeholders, and treatment descriptions.
  - **Subtle Tertiary (`#8E968D`):** Captions, disabled text, and fine-print disclaimers.

## Typography

The typographical strategy employs an intentional dialogue between classic editorial grace and clinical modernity:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Conveys bespoke craftsmanship, warmth, and prestige. Display headlines lean toward lighter weights (`400`–`500`) with tight line heights to maintain an editorial magazine posture. Italicized styling is used sparingly for curated emphasis (e.g., treatment names, personal sign-offs).
- **Body & Clinical Descriptions (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, open letterforms engineered for effortless readability across long descriptions, ingredient transparency lists, and diagnostic evaluations.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase settings with generous letter-spacing (`0.15em`) are used for category tags, protocol numbers, and luxury badges to establish visual hierarchy without heavy weight.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model mimics high-fashion editorial layouts, emphasizing unhurried vertical cadence and asymmetrical compositions over dense information packing.

### Grid & Layout Rules
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 12-column grid with a maximum content canvas width of `1280px` centered on wide viewports. Margin is `4rem`, and gutters are `2rem`.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Standard 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Whitespace Principles:** Sections breathe through expanded vertical gaps (`space-2xl` and above). Content modules should never feel congested; clinical efficacy is framed by quiet spatial buffers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids conventional harsh drop shadows in favor of ambient luminescence, frosted diffusion, and layered hairline planes:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Luminous Canvas (`#FCFCFB`).
- **Level 1 (Surface Layer):** Soft Sand tint (`#F7F5F0`) paired with a `1px` continuous border in `#E5E0D8`. No shadow.
- **Level 2 (Glassmorphic Floating Cards & Badges):** 
  - Background: `rgba(252, 252, 251, 0.72)`.
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px) saturate(140%)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(229, 224, 216, 0.6)`.
  - Shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(91, 107, 90, 0.06), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(30, 34, 30, 0.02)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Concierge Drawers, Popovers):**
  - Background: `#FCFCFB`.
  - Border: `1px solid #E5E0D8`.
  - Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(30, 34, 30, 0.08), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(91, 107, 90, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture balances sleek pill geometries for interactive elements with softly rounded, architectural boundaries for visual containers:

- **Interactive Controls (Pill Standard):** Buttons, tags, protocol pills, and form trigger toggles utilize full rounded caps (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to express organic softness and skin-friendly tactility.
- **Content Cards & Panels:** Medium roundedness (`1rem` to `1.5rem`) to maintain architectural balance with editorial typography.
- **Hero Image Masks & Clinical Imagery:** Softly rounded corners (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) or subtle arched crowns referencing classical spa archways.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill (`9999px`), solid Sage Green (`#5B6B5A`) background, off-white text (`#FCFCFB`). Hover transition subtly lightens surface to `#7C8D7B` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(91, 107, 90, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary (Outline):** Full pill, transparent background, `1px` hairline border in `#5B6B5A`, text in `#5B6B5A`. On hover, fills with light sage tint (`rgba(124, 141, 123, 0.08)`).
- **Luxury Accent:** Full pill, Champagne Gold accent outline or soft fill (`#D4AF37`) with deep slate typography (`#1E221E`) for bespoke scheduling or VIP clinical pathways.
- **Text / Ghost Action:** Understated text in `#1E221E` with an animated sliding hairline underline (`#D4AF37`) beneath it.

### Chips & Protocol Badges
- **Editorial Badges:** Small full-pill elements, glassmorphic backdrop (`rgba(247, 245, 240, 0.85)`), `1px` border (`#E5E0D8`), label-uppercase styling in `#636B62`.
- **Selected Filter Chips:** Deep sage background (`#5B6B5A`), white typography, zero border.

### Cards
- **Treatment & Clinical Cards:** Surface in `#F7F5F0` or frosted off-white, framed by a continuous `1px` border in `#E5E0D8`. Padded generously (`space-xl`). Imagery maintains 4:5 or 3:4 portrait ratios with `rounded-lg` radius.
- **Floating Feature Overlay Cards:** Glassmorphic translucent canvas positioned overlapping imagery with ambient botanical shadow.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Pill-shaped or soft-rounded (`rounded-lg`) fields with Warm Nude backgrounds (`#F7F5F0`). Border is `1px solid #E5E0D8`. Text in `#1E221E` with muted olive placeholders (`#636B62`).
- **Focus State:** Hairline border smoothly transitions to `#5B6B5A` with a soft `0 0 0 3px rgba(91, 107, 90, 0.12)` halo.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded square (`0.375rem`), `1px` border in `#C5A059` or `#5B6B5A`. Checked state fills with `#5B6B5A` displaying a pure white check icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric circles; outer ring `#E5E0D8`, checked state uses `#5B6B5A` ring enclosing a solid central dot.

### Specialized System Components
- **Clinical Step / Protocol Tracker:** Minimalist horizontal hairline timeline with Champagne Gold numeral markers and Playfair step headers.
- **Diagnostic Result / Consultation Bar:** Soft dual-toned progress bars utilizing Sage-to-Champagne muted gradients over soft-sand tracks.
